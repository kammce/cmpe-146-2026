By: Khalil Estell

Visualizations

  • PWM Simulator
    Interactive pulse-width modulation visualization: progressive, stationary, and reactive (RC filter) signal modes.
  • Hardware Timer Simulator
    Counter, load register, and PWM compare-channel visualization.
  • Pulse Capture Simulator
    Reset-on-rising, capture-on-falling pulse width measurement.
  • SPI Bus Visualizer
    Controller sharing one bus with a temp sensor and a GPIO expander (each on its own CS line) — write/read transactions on a logic-analyzer style trace, with selectable SPI clock modes.
  • SPI Shift Register Visualizer
    The hardware underneath every SPI transfer: a PISO shift register on the controller feeding a SIPO shift register on the peripheral, one animated bit per clock.
  • UART Baud Mismatch Visualizer
    Two microcontrollers with independent baud rates and no shared clock line — watch the receiver's sample points drift off-center and corrupt data (or trigger a framing error) as the mismatch grows.
